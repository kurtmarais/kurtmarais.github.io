abstract: |
  This research investigates how affective information diffuses through online social networks and its implications for individuals with depression, situated within the frameworks of emotional contagion and infodemiology. Using a large-scale Reddit dataset, the work develops a depression-specific sentiment lexicon, applies topic modelling to identify key themes in online mental health discourse, and uses agent-based simulation of a Twitter/X network to test individual- and group-level interventions for improving well-being.

  A central finding is evidence of *emotional reinforcement* rather than traditional person-to-person emotional contagion: socially connected individuals who share the same diagnosis and emotional state tend to experience intensified emotions together, for longer and more frequently than other pairings — with particular relevance for individuals experiencing depression.
