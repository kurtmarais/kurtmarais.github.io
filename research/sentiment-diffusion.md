## Research question

How do individual sentiment states change as a function of social network structure, neighbouring sentiment, temporal dependence and individual characteristics?

## Approach

The programme combines social network analysis, natural language processing, sentiment measurement, longitudinal analysis and agent-based modelling to examine affective diffusion as a dynamic network process.

## Methods

**Network:** Directed social network  
**States:** Positive · Neutral · Negative  
**Methods:** NLP · SNA · ABM · time-series analysis
