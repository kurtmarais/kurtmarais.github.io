I am a lecturer and researcher working in computational social science, with research interests spanning social network analysis, sentiment diffusion, information diffusion, natural language processing and agent-based modelling.

My research examines how information, sentiment and behaviour move through connected social systems, with particular attention to the interaction between network structure, individual states and temporal dynamics.
