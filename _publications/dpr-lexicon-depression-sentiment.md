description: "The lexicon was constructed from four general-purpose sentiment lexica and augmented with corpus data obtained from the r/depression subreddit. It may be used to support sentiment analysis of social media posts related to experiences of depression and may be implemented in the same way as other domain-independent lexica. The sentiment score of a document is determined by adding the sentiment scores of the words it contains. The lexicon is sensitive to text concerning depression, although it may also be sensitive to co-occurring mental health concerns such as anxiety."
