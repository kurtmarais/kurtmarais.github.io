My research investigates information, sentiment and behavioural dynamics in connected social systems using computational and quantitative methods.

## Sentiment diffusion and emotional contagion

Research examining how sentiment states propagate through social networks, including the role of network structure, neighbouring states, temporal dependence and individual characteristics.

## Information diffusion

Computational approaches to understanding how information spreads through digitally mediated networks and how network position influences diffusion processes.

## Agent-based modelling of social processes

Simulation models linking individual-level states and rules with network structure to investigate emergent population-level dynamics.

## Computational methods

Natural language processing, social network analysis, time-series analysis, simulation and quantitative modelling for computational social science.
